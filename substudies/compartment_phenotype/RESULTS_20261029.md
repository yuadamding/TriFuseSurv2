## Bounded paired design assessment

Each scenario has exactly 1,000 saved replicates. The approximation preserves
the 150 development covariate rows and calibrates exponential event/censoring
rates using their 75 deaths and observed-duration median of 3,214 days. It
does not fit real phenotype–OS coefficients.

| Scenario | Usable | Rejection / power | Coverage | Median log-HR interval width | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Null compartment difference | 1,000 | 7.70% | 93.20% | 0.7950 | Rejection exceeds 7% limit |
| HR 1.5 per fixed development SD of D | 999 | 49.55% power | 93.59% | 0.9978 | Width exceeds 0.81093 limit |
| Common-M temporal stress, D effects zero | 1,000 | 8.50% | 95.00% | 1.9532, nongating | Rejection exceeds 7% limit |

Coverage and usable-fit point criteria pass, but the overall design decision
fails. Monte Carlo rates have sampling uncertainty; the frozen point-estimate
rules were not replaced with a different seed or revised thresholds.
The temporal guard tests D-late in the seven/eight-slope model. Its result
does not validate the misspecified six-slope PH model; that separate
diagnostic rejected in 21/999 temporal-stress fits.

No primary association lock or real coefficient-difference result was issued.
